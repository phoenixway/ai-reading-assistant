CHAPTER 1

On the rooftop Vero examined the cracked antenna. It seemed to lean farther east than yesterday, though no measurement had been taken.

Hal said, “The storm bent it last night.” Vero replied, “Maybe. I don't know that yet.” She photographed the antenna base and marked a chalk line beside it.

Later Hal claimed the mast had shifted again. Vero measured the distance to the chalk line and found no change. She remained on the rooftop.
