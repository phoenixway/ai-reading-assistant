CHAPTER 1

Priya unfolded a survey map across the truck hood. Along the northern margin, blue-pencil words said: “North culvert blocked. Use ridge path.” Priya traced the ridge path with one finger.

Malik asked whether the culvert was safe. Priya answered, “We take the ridge.” She circled checkpoint C in red pencil and folded the map.

They reached the ridge path beneath marker C. Priya placed the map in her pack while Malik checked the compass.
