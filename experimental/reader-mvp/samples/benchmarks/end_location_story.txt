CHAPTER 1

Ada and Bruno ate breakfast in the kitchen. Bruno said he needed to repair the pump in the workshop. Ada said she would stay inside and check the household ledger.

Bruno took his wrench and left for the workshop. Ada remained in the kitchen, opened the wall cabinet, and removed a green ledger.

A few minutes later Bruno called from the workshop phone. Ada answered from the kitchen, wrote “pump belt” in the ledger, returned the ledger to the cabinet, and stayed in the kitchen.
