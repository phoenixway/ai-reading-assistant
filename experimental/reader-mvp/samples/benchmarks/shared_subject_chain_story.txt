CHAPTER 1

Elena reached the floodgate platform. She unlatched the maintenance hatch, descended the iron ladder, retrieved a sealed canister from the lower shelf, and climbed back to the platform.

Without stopping, Elena crossed the catwalk and returned the canister to Tomas. Tomas tucked it beneath his coat.

Elena stayed at the floodgate platform beside the open hatch. Tomas disappeared into the turbine corridor with the canister under his coat.
