CHAPTER 1

Jonas carried a clipboard into the control room and gave it to Mira. The top sheet was an incident report headed “Generator 4”. In red ink someone had written: “Generator 4 failed at 02:10. Do not restart.”

Mira read the warning silently, copied 02:10 into her notebook, and folded the report in half. Jonas asked what it said. “It says we wait for the mechanic,” Mira replied.

Before dawn Mira placed the folded report under the radio and told Jonas, “Call Dena when the mechanic arrives.” She remained in the control room after Jonas left for the loading bay.
