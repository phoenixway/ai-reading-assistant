CHAPTER 1

Maeve set a brass compass on a crate beside Ivo, a man in a dark coat. He snatched it, wrapped it in a scarf, and tossed it across the room to Lian, a woman waiting by the window. She caught the compass and slid it into her satchel.

A moment later Lian pulled the compass out, passed it to Oren, a man standing beside the west passage, and zipped the satchel shut. He clipped the compass to his belt and stepped into the west passage.

Maeve remained beside the crate. Oren stopped at the west passage entrance with the compass visible on his belt.
