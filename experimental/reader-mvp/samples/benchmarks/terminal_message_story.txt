CHAPTER 1

At 06:40 Jun woke the wall terminal. Its display flashed: “Pump array offline. Manual reset required.” Jun copied fault code X9-14 onto scrap paper.

Selene entered the operations bay and asked what happened. Jun answered, “The pumps are down.” He left the display untouched and did not read its warning aloud.

Jun shut the terminal, pocketed the paper, and remained in the operations bay while Selene went to the pump room.
