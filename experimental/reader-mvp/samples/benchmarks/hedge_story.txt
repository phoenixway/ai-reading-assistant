CHAPTER 1

At noon Keva crossed the station hall with a canvas bag. Rian watched from the ticket booth while she stopped beside the locked service door and checked the handle once.

Keva tilted her head as if she had heard a sound behind the door. She waited a few seconds, then walked to the platform. Rian later said, “She looked frightened to me,” but Keva said nothing.

A train arrived. Keva boarded the rear carriage, placed the canvas bag beneath her seat, and opened a paperback. Rian left through the front doors. Keva stayed aboard the rear carriage as the train departed.
