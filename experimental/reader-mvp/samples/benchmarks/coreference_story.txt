CHAPTER 1

Nadia placed a silver key on the kitchen table. “This opens the archive room, not the cellar,” she told Olek. Olek thanked her but left the key where it was.

After Nadia went upstairs, Olek picked up the silver key. He wrapped it in a blue handkerchief, put it in his left coat pocket, and locked the table drawer before leaving the kitchen.

At dusk Olek met Petro beside the courtyard gate. He handed Petro the silver key and said, “Give this only to Nadia.” Petro nodded, hid the key inside his boot, and stayed by the gate while Olek walked away.
