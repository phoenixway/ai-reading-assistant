CHAPTER 1

Lora held the workshop door while Dax carried a wooden crate inside. “Put it by the furnace,” Lora said.

Dax started, “I thought the east room—” but Lora interrupted, “Not tonight.” He set the crate beside the furnace.

From the hallway Eren called, “Dax, come here.” Dax answered, “One minute,” and stayed in the workshop beside the crate.
