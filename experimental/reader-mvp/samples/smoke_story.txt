CHAPTER 1

Mara had told Levin twice that the eastern stair was sealed. On the third evening she found him testing the brass handle anyway. “My father welded it shut before he died,” she said. Levin stepped back, embarrassed, and apologized.

The rain began after midnight. A courier named Sava arrived soaked through and handed Mara a narrow grey envelope. On the front someone had written B17 in blue pencil. Mara slipped it into her coat without opening it.

***

At breakfast Levin asked whether the courier had brought news. “Only an invoice,” Mara said. She kept her eyes on the stove. Sava, already leaving, paused as if he wanted to correct her, then thought better of it.

Mara waited until Levin went to the workshop. She opened the envelope. Inside was a railway receipt and a short note: The eastern stair was opened yesterday. Come alone. She read it twice, burned the note, and kept the receipt.

CHAPTER 2

That afternoon Levin came back early. He noticed ash in the cold stove and the corner of a blue railway receipt under Mara's notebook. He said nothing. When Mara asked why he was staring, he smiled and offered to make tea.

A crash sounded behind the wall. Mara ran first. The supposedly sealed eastern door stood open by a hand's width. Levin caught her sleeve before she touched it. “You knew,” he said. Mara answered, “I suspected.”

They heard footsteps below. Mara gave Levin the brass key she had worn on a string under her shirt since childhood. “If I don't come back, locker B17 at Central Station.” Levin stared at the key, then closed his fist around it.

***

Mara went down the eastern stair alone. Levin waited exactly three minutes before following her, despite the promise he had just made. At the bottom he found Sava unconscious and Mara kneeling beside him. A stranger was running toward the service yard.

Levin could have chased the stranger. Instead he helped Mara carry Sava upstairs. For the first time, Mara told Levin that her father had not died in an accident. She said he had been killed because of what was hidden in locker B17.
